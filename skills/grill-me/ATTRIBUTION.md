# Attributions and Citations

Adapted from Matt Pocock's [`grilling` skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md), which defines the design tree, the frontier worked in rounds, the numbered-question-with-recommended-answer format, and the facts-are-yours / decisions-are-theirs split.

This variant shifts the aim from relentless stress-testing to elicitation and alignment, makes the recommended answer a deliberate elicitation tool — placed or withheld per question — and adds the don't-lead-the-witness guardrails, evaluable round sizing, the closing settled-decisions artifact, and the crossroute to `brainstorming`.
