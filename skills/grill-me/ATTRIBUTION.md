# Attributions and Citations

Adapted from Matt Pocock's [`grilling` skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md), which defines the design tree, the frontier worked in rounds, the numbered-question-with-recommended-answer format, and the facts-are-yours / decisions-are-theirs split.

This variant shifts the aim from relentless stress-testing to elicitation and alignment, withholds the agent's view until the user answers and then requires an explicit agree-or-disagree verdict against it, shapes each question to its decision space instead of a fixed option list, and adds the don't-lead-the-witness guardrails, evaluable round sizing, the closing settled-decisions artifact, and the crossroute to `brainstorming`.
